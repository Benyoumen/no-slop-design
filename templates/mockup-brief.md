## The job

In the words of the person who does it: "<what they are trying to get done, when, under what pressure>".

## The person

Who opens this, how often, on which device, with how much time. What they already know. What they are afraid of getting wrong.

## The number

The outcome this screen is meant to move, with today's value and the target: <metric, from X to Y, by when>.

## What the screen shows

The three to five things the person needs to see first, in order. The source of each (system, field, snapshot date).

## What the person can do

The main action, from start to result. The secondary action. The way back (undo, cancel).

## What the screen must never do

Three to five lines. Example: never write to the CRM without the rep's approval; never show a number the source does not have.

## If an agent acts

Autonomy tier of each action: suggests, proposes, acts with approval, acts alone. What the person sees of the agent's presence, proposals and trail.

## Data snapshot

File, date, source system, anonymization rule (kept private), fields with no data.

## States to show in version 1

Empty, loading, error (which one), success (which one).

## Audience of the validation session

Participants (roles), the decider, the one who is not technical.
